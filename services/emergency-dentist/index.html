Good to know

Questions about urgent visits

Do you accept walk-ins?

Please call before coming in. Calling lets us assess urgency, reserve appropriate time, and tell you if another care setting is safer.

What should I do with a knocked-out adult tooth?

Hold it by the crown, avoid touching the root, gently rinse without scrubbing, and call a dentist immediately. If possible, place it back in the socket or keep it moist in milk.

When should I seek emergency medical care?

Call emergency services for trouble breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, major facial trauma, or rapidly spreading swelling. Those symptoms may need hospital care.

Can you promise a same-day appointment?

No. Availability changes throughout the day. We will tell you the soonest option and help identify an appropriate alternative if needed.
