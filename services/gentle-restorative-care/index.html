Good to know

Questions about restorative care

How do I know whether I need a filling or crown?

The choice depends on how much healthy tooth remains, where the damage sits, and how the tooth handles pressure. We show you what we see and explain the tradeoffs.

What do you do for dental anxiety?

We agree on a pause signal, explain steps before they happen, use local anesthetic when appropriate, and build in time for questions.

Can treatment be spread across visits?

Often, yes. When clinically safe, we can prioritize urgent needs and phase other care around your schedule and budget.

Do you guarantee a treatment result?

No dental treatment can honestly be guaranteed. We discuss expected outcomes, alternatives, and follow-up before you decide.
