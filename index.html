Your first visit

No guessing.
Here’s what happens.

It is okay to feel nervous. We build time into a first appointment to listen, answer questions, and agree on a pace before any exam begins.

  1. 01

    Tell us what matters

    Share what brought you in, what makes visits difficult, and how much detail you want.

  2. 02

    Set your pace

    Choose a pause signal. Ask for quiet, explanations, or breaks—your preferences lead.

  3. 03

    You get written priorities and next steps. Nothing moves ahead without your say-so.

Good to know

Questions, answered plainly

Do I need to know what treatment I need before booking?

No. Tell us what you are noticing or what you hope to change. The first visit is for understanding your needs and deciding on next steps together.

Can I ask for extra time or breaks?

Yes. Let the team know when you book, and we will note your preferences. You can also use an agreed pause signal at any point during a visit.

Does the online request confirm my appointment?

No. It sends your preferred times to the team. We will contact you to confirm availability and appointment details.

Do you offer an online insurance checker?

No. Coverage varies by plan and treatment. Call the practice and we can help you understand what information to ask your insurer for before care.
